## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Tips

- **"Chrome not found" error:** Install Google Chrome from the official site and try again.
- **Stuck at login:** Make sure you're logged into X in your normal Chrome window first. Then restart x-post.
- **Video not uploading:** Check your internet speed, and ensure the video file is not corrupted. Also, keep videos under 2 minutes for fastest results.
- **Blank screen:** Close x-post completely, reopen it, and try again. If the issue persists, restart your computer.

Most issues are solved by simply restarting the app or your PC.
